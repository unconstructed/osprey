# Swap costs on Base: first sampling (2026-11-25)

Read-only measurement, approved by the owner on 2026-11-25 (AGENTS.md "Data"). Nothing in `config/` was changed;
the block at the end is a **proposal** for the owner (hard rule 10).

## Method

- **What:** USDC↔WETH and USDC↔cbBTC, both directions, at $100, $1,000, $10,000 and $50,000 (sized at the Coinbase
  mid). Each sample asks 0x (`GET /swap/permit2/price`, no taker) and Uniswap v3 directly (QuoterV2
  `quoteExactInputSingle` at fee tiers 1, 5 and 30 bps; the best one counts). Both are read on the same block, the
  one 0x quoted at. The reference is the Coinbase BTC-USD or ETH-USD mid at that moment, with USDC counted at $1.
- **When:** 11 rounds, 10 minutes apart, 18:05 to 19:45 UTC on Wednesday 25 November 2026. That is 176 samples
  (352 priced rows, 44 per size and route) from 1,498 requests, all answered (no failures, retries or incomplete
  0x simulations).
- **Cost** = (USD value sold − USD value received) / value sold, both at the Coinbase mid, plus gas. The split is
  0x's own fee (`fees.zeroExFee`), or the pool's fee tier on the direct route, then the rest (pool fees inside
  0x, price impact, and the gap between the Base and Coinbase prices).
- **Gas:** for 0x, its `totalNetworkFee` (L1 data fee included). For the direct route, QuoterV2's `gasEstimate`
  plus 81,000 (21,000 for the transaction plus a stated 60,000 allowance for the router and transfers; not
  measured) at the node's `eth_gasPrice`, plus Base's L1 fee upper bound for a 400-byte transaction. Both are valued
  at the ETH mid.
- **Verified on chain first.** The tokens' code, `symbol()` and `decimals()` matched. The Uniswap v3 factory
  `0x3312…FDfD` and QuoterV2 `0x3d4e…B76a` have code. QuoterV2's `factory()` and `WETH9()` match. `getPool` returns
  a pool with code at every tier, and each pool's factory, fee and tokens match. A $100 quote landed 0.3 bps (WETH)
  and 3.4 bps (cbBTC) from the Coinbase mid. Pool addresses are in the raw `verification.json`.
- **Code and data:** `lab/data/swap_costs.py` and `tests/test_swap_costs.py`. Raw files are in
  `data/raw/swap_costs/20261125T180503Z/` (manifest SHA-256 `ab51b14a…`; sampler code `d0afb3ce…`). The table
  `data/swap_costs/20261125T180503Z.parquet` was rebuilt from the raw files with the final code (`533b9332…`).

## Results: total cost in bps (median / worst), all four directions and both coins pooled, n = 44 each

| Size | 0x total | 0x fee | Direct total | Direct cheaper in | 0x gas, median / worst | Direct gas, worst |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| $100 | 17.5 / 188.7 | 15.0 | 2.9 / 9.9 | 44 of 44 samples | $0.009 / $0.096 | $0.003 |
| $1,000 | 16.8 / 149.1 | 15.0 | 4.0 / 9.9 | 44 of 44 | $0.016 / $0.120 | $0.003 |
| $10,000 | 16.6 / 177.3 | 15.0 | 8.6 / 15.3 | 44 of 44 | $0.029 / $0.099 | $0.003 |
| $50,000 | 16.8 / 37.1 | 15.0 | 22.1 / 31.3 | 7 of 44 | $0.044 / $0.102 | $0.003 |

By coin (median / worst total): WETH direct costs 1.5 / 7.9 bps at $100, 9.7 / 15.3 at $10k and 22.8 / 31.3 at $50k;
cbBTC direct costs 5.9 / 9.9, 8.3 / 12.5 and 20.3 / 25.5. The 0x medians are 16 to 18 bps for both coins at every
size. In the direct route the 1 bp WETH pool wins up to $1k, and the 5 bps pool wins otherwise (sometimes the 30 bps
pool, for $50k WETH sales).

**Which is cheaper:** direct Uniswap v3 at $100 to $10,000 in every sample, by a median 14.4, 13.3 and 8.2 bps
(mostly 0x's 15 bps fee). At $50,000, 0x was cheaper in 37 of 44 samples, by a median 4.4 bps: a single pool's
impact grows with size, while 0x splits the trade.

**0x's worst cases all come from one source.** In the 29 samples whose route used "LunarBase", 0x cost a median
39 bps beyond its fee (worst 173). In the 147 without it: a median 1.0 bps (worst 8.6). Without LunarBase, the 0x
total is 17.3 / 23.6 bps at $100, 15.7 / 18.9 at $1k, 15.9 / 19.3 at $10k and 16.8 / 23.6 at $50k.

**Base vs Coinbase gap** (the 5 bps pool's mid against the Coinbase mid, 88 samples per coin; positive means Base
is dearer): cbBTC median +0.5 bps, range −5.2 to +5.2; WETH median +1.0, range −3.8 to +7.4. A 5 bps pool's mid
can sit up to about its fee away from the market, so this is good to about ±5 bps.

## Against the placeholders in `config/costs.yaml`

| Placeholder | Measured |
|---|---|
| `pool_fee_bps: 5` | Direct: the best tier was 1 bp (WETH, $1k and under) or 5 bps. On the 0x route the pool fees are inside 0x's price and not reported |
| `aggregator_fee_bps: 0` | **Wrong: 0x charged 15.0 bps on every sample** (a "volume" fee, in USDC) |
| `slippage_bps` 10 / 15 / 30 at $100 / $1k / $10k | Cost beyond the fee, for 0x: median 1.2 / 1.5 / 1.6 bps, 90th percentile 30 / 53 / 25 (LunarBase), 2.5 / 2.4 / 2.7 without it. Direct price impact: median 0.0 / 1.8 / 3.6 bps, and 16 at $50k |
| `gas_usd_per_trade: 0.10` | 0x: median $0.02, 90th percentile $0.066, **worst $0.12** (gas grows with the number of route hops). Direct: at most $0.003 |

All in, the placeholders (25 / 21 / 35 bps at $100 / $1k / $10k) sit above the 0x medians (17.5 / 16.8 / 16.6) but
below 0x's 90th percentiles (45 / 68 / 40), and well above the direct route (2.9 / 4.0 / 8.6).

## Proposed `config/costs.yaml` (PROPOSAL, for the owner; not applied)

For the route production uses today (0x, every source allowed). The numbers are the 90th percentile of the 44
samples per size, rounded up; the 2x stress run covers the tails.

```yaml
# config/costs.yaml — PROPOSED from reports/costs/2026-11-25.md (one session, 176 samples). Owner to approve.
venue: base_via_0x
pool_fee_bps: 0            # inside 0x's price; counted in slippage_bps
aggregator_fee_bps: 15     # 0x volume fee, measured on every sample
slippage_bps:              # 90th percentile of cost beyond 0x's fee, by trade size in USD
  "100": 30
  "1000": 54
  "10000": 26
  "50000": 5
gas_usd_per_trade: 0.15    # worst measured $0.12, plus margin for busier hours
stress_multiplier: 2.0     # unchanged
```

Alternatives, if the owner changes the routing:
- **0x without LunarBase** (would need 0x's `excludedSources`; untested): slippage 3 / 3 / 3 / 5, gas 0.15.
- **Direct Uniswap v3:** `pool_fee_bps: 0`, `aggregator_fee_bps: 0`, slippage (90th percentile of the whole cost)
  9 / 9 / 13 / 26, gas 0.01.

## Limits

- **One session:** about 100 minutes on one weekday evening (UTC), not "a few days". No weekend, Asian hours or
  stressed market.
- **No taker address:** 0x simulates from a default address. A firm quote for our own wallet could route
  differently, LunarBase especially.
- **Narrow direct route:** a single Uniswap v3 pool per trade, with no splitting, no Uniswap v4 and no Aerodrome.
  A real direct router would do at least as well.
- **Gas allowance:** the direct route's gas includes an estimated 60,000 for the router (not measured). Gas is tiny
  either way.
- **One-time approvals** (Permit2, token allowances) are not counted.

## Repeating it over several days

Run `uv run python -m lab.data.swap_costs sample --rounds 36 --interval-min 40` once a day for 7 days, including a
weekend (about 576 0x requests a day, far under the limit). Each run gets its own folder; then use
`... summary data/swap_costs/*.parquet`. Add `excludedSources=LunarBase` as a second 0x request per sample, after the
owner agrees, to price the "0x without LunarBase" option on the same blocks.
