## 3. Luck control: the same search on shuffled data

Shuffling the daily moves destroys every real pattern. The search still found an in-sample Sharpe of 1.30. Out of sample: Sharpe -0.75, DSR 0.00, PBO 0.70. This is what a false winner looks like, and why the gates exist.
