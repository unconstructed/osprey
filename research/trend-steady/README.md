# trend-steady (SCOUTING)
- What: a weekly trend rule for Steady (BTC and ETH each move to USDC in a downtrend), with a customer crypto-share setting of 30-60% (Branch R, 35% drawdown budget) and a stop-loss variant at a 20%, 30% or 40% fall from the wallet's high. The share is bought at entry; rising prices can push crypto to about 80% of the wallet before a limit fix.
